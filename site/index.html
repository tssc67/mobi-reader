LESS BETWEEN YOU AND THE PAGE

A reader that gives
your books room.

01

A library of your own

Bring your EPUB and DRM-free legacy MOBI books together. Search, sort, and pick up where you left off. Your originals stay untouched.

02

Settle into the page

Choose paper, sepia, or night. Adjust type size, line spacing, and reading width. Save a bookmark for the passages you want to return to.

03

Keep it close

Your books and reading progress stay on your computer. No account, no cloud library, and no Calibre installation required.

A few things to know.

This is an early release. It reads reflowable EPUB 2/3 and legacy MOBI, with scrollable chapters and simplified book styling. DRM, pure KF8, PDF, and fixed-layout books are not supported.

The published Windows download is currently unsigned. Linux builds are available from source; see the build instructions and included license notices before redistributing.
